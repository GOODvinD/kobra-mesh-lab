04 / HOW IT WORKS

From printer to insight

01Run full auto-leveling

Install the PEI plate. On the printer touchscreen choose Control → Smart assist → Calibration → Auto levelling, tick the checkbox, then press Start. Wait until the calibration finishes before exporting.

02Export and preserve the log

Once leveling has finished, insert a USB drive and choose Settings → General → Export Log. Copy AC_LOG*.pack to your computer. A new export can overwrite the previous file on USB; rename or archive each copy.

03Pick the calibration you want

If bed calibration was run several times during the day, one exported log can contain multiple recorded meshes with their own timestamps. Use the Calibration snapshot dropdown to choose the exact dataset you want before comparing.

1. Run a full 49-point auto-level

Install the PEI sheet, then open Control → Smart assist → Calibration → Auto levelling. Tick the checkbox and press Start; wait until all 49 probing points are finished. Do not touch the hot bed or moving toolhead.

2. Export to USB and save a copy

Insert USB after leveling, choose Settings → General → Export Log, and copy the .pack to your PC. A later export can overwrite the USB file, so rename each saved copy.

3. Choose another timestamp if needed

Some logs contain multiple recorded meshes from different calibration runs on the same day. Use the dropdown to switch to another calibration snapshot and timestamp.

Measurement notes

Values are firmware-reported relative Z heights / compensation values (mm), not absolute nozzle-to-bed clearances. If you want the freshest result, run auto-calibration immediately before exporting the log. A raw difference includes reference/Z-zero shifts and may not mean the physical bed changed. “Remove mean shift” compares spatial variation; “Remove mean & tilt” isolates local shape changes. Use identical bed temperatures and repeat probing for reliable comparisons. XY orientation follows the coordinates recorded in the log; verify physical corner orientation before mechanical adjustments. Not affiliated with Anycubic.
